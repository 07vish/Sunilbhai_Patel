Common questions.

What should I bring for my income tax return?
  • PAN and Aadhaar
  • Form 16 or details of your income
  • Bank statements and interest certificates
  • Form 26AS or AIS, if you have it
  • Investment, insurance and loan statements

The exact list depends on your case. Call us and we will confirm it.

I received an income tax notice. What now?

Do not ignore it. Note the reply date written on the notice and bring it to the office with your related papers.

Do you handle business accounts too?

Yes. We keep accounts and prepare tax filings for individuals, traders and firms.

Can you notarise my documents?

Haresh N. Patel is an Advocate and Notary. Carry the original documents and your ID, and call before you visit.
