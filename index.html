Common questions.

What should I bring for my income tax return? +

For most individual filings, please bring:

  • PAN and Aadhaar card (duly linked)
  • Form 16 or details of your salary income
  • Bank account statements for the financial year
  • Form 26AS and AIS (Annual Information Statement)
  • Investment, life insurance, and loan interest statements

The exact list depends on your specific sources of income. Call us and we will confirm it beforehand.

I received an income tax notice. What should I do? +

Do not ignore it. Statutory notices have strict deadlines for response. Note the reply date specified on the notice, and bring it to our Varachha Road office along with your related financial papers. We will evaluate the matter and draft an appropriate legal reply.

Do you manage ongoing accounting for commercial firms? +

Yes. We manage regular bookkeeping, ledger reconciliation, balance sheet finalization, and quarterly GST filings for traders, manufacturers, and partnership firms.

Can you notarize documents at the office? +

Haresh N. Patel is a practicing Advocate and certified Government Notary. Please carry your original instruments and government photo identification, and call before you visit to ensure availability.
